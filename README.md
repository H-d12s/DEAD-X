# Top-Down Zombie Shooter 🚗🧟

## Overview

**Top-Down Zombie Shooter** is an action survival game where the player controls a **weaponized car** in a zombie-infested city. The car is equipped with guns to fight off waves of zombies while completing objectives in each level.

The game combines **driving mechanics, shooting, and survival gameplay**, with each level introducing a different challenge.

---

## Gameplay

* Control a **car equipped with guns**.
* Drive around the map while **shooting zombies**.
* Avoid getting surrounded by zombies.
* Complete **different objectives in each level** to progress.

---

## Levels

### Level 1 – Package Delivery 📦

The city is overrun by zombies, but deliveries still need to be made.

**Objective:**

* Drive around the map.
* Pick up packages.
* Deliver them to the correct delivery spots.
* Avoid or shoot zombies that try to stop you.

---

### Level 2 – Escape the Giant Zombie 🧟‍♂️

A massive green zombie appears and starts chasing you.

**Objective:**

* Survive while being chased by the giant zombie.
* Dodge obstacles and smaller zombies.
* Escape the area before the giant zombie catches you.

---

### Level 3 – Boss Fight 💀

The final challenge is a **boss battle**.

**Objective:**

* Fight a powerful zombie boss.
* Avoid its attacks.
* Use your weapons to defeat it and survive.

---

## Features

* Top-down driving mechanics
* Car-mounted guns
* Multiple levels with different objectives
* Zombie enemies with increasing difficulty
* Boss fight in the final level
